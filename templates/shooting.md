# Shooting rules

These make the automatic edit reliable. Break them and the editor guesses.

1. **Vertical, 1080x1920, 30 fps.** iPhone: turn OFF HDR video (Settings > Camera > Record Video >
   HDR Video). HDR footage turns washed out and grey after editing.
2. **Mic close to your mouth.** A clip-on mic beats the phone mic every time. Quiet room, no music
   playing while you record.
3. **Made a mistake? Pause one second, then say the WHOLE line again from the start.** Do not patch
   from the middle of a sentence. The editor keeps your last complete take of every line.
4. **One second of silence between lines.** That gives clean cut points.
5. **Say the script, roughly.** Small wording changes are fine. Big improvisations get flagged as
   "not in the script" and left out unless you ask to keep them.
6. **Effects need room.** If the brief says "headroom" for a line, leave space above your head.
   Keep your face roughly in the upper-middle of the frame. Plain background is better than busy.
7. One long recording or several files are both fine. Several files are used in name order.
